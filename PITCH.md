# Kwality Printer: Online Growth Pitch

**Business:** Kwality Printer, 26 Padgate Lane, Warrington WA1 3RX · 01925 637955
**Demo:** `demo/index.html` (shop) and `demo/admin.html` (staff dashboard)

---

## 1. Where they are today

| What works | What's holding them back |
|---|---|
| Very strong prices (1,000 cards £49, 10,000 leaflets £99, 10,000 menus £349) | The website looks 15+ years old and isn't built for phones |
| Wide range: stationery, menus, posters, stands | Customers can't get a price, upload artwork or pay online, so every order needs a call or email |
| Free artwork, free proof, 24-hour printing and free delivery are already offered | Those strengths are buried in a sidebar |
| Takeaway menus are a clear niche | No reorder option for takeaways, who reprint menus every few months |
| | No reviews, no examples of past work, and Gmail addresses make the business look less established |
| | Skype and Yahoo chat icons show the site hasn't been updated in years |

## 2. What the demo shows

1. **Instant quote for 19 products.** Customers choose size, paper, sides, quantity and turnaround, and the price updates as they go. It shows the price per item and an estimated ready date.
2. **Artwork upload with an automatic check.** Customers drag and drop a PDF, JPG or Word file, and the site checks the file type and size and warns if an image is low resolution.
3. **Basket and checkout.** Customers choose collection, free UK delivery, a same-day local courier or next-day delivery, then pick card, PayPal, bank transfer or pay on collection.
4. **Built-in upsells:**
   - *"Get 20,000 for £X more, 14% cheaper per item"*: suggests the next quantity up when it's better value
   - *"Customers also order…"*: suggests a matching product
   - *"Spend £Y more for 250 free business cards"*: a progress bar for their existing offer
   - Promo codes (`WELCOME10`, `MENU20`)
5. **Bundles:** a Starter Pack, a Takeaway Launch Pack and an Event Pack, which raise the average order value.
6. **Track and one-click reorder.** Customers enter an order number or email, see the job's progress and reorder everything in one click.
7. **Trade account sign-up**, to capture regular B2B customers.
8. **WhatsApp everywhere.** Customers can send a quote to the shop's WhatsApp, and staff can send a customer their proof or a "ready for collection" message in one tap.
9. **Local search basics:** a Warrington-focused page title, business details marked up for Google, a list of nearby areas served and an FAQ.
10. **Staff dashboard:**
    - Revenue, average order, jobs in progress, repeat customers and the share of quotes that become orders
    - Order list with search, status updates and a due date (rush jobs flagged)
    - Today's to-do list: proofs to send, artwork to chase, collections to text
    - Revenue by product
    - **Quotes to follow up**: people who priced a job but didn't buy
    - Leads, plus a CSV export for accounts

## 3. Where the extra revenue comes from

These are illustrative assumptions to discuss with the owner, not forecasts.

| Lever | Assumption | Extra per month |
|---|---|---|
| New customers who order online after hours or without phoning | 20 extra orders × £90 average | ~£1,800 |
| Higher average order from upsells and bundles | 10% on £8k monthly sales | ~£800 |
| Takeaway reorder reminders | 15 takeaways reorder once more per year × £349 | ~£440 |
| Chasing quotes that didn't turn into orders | 10 of 50 quotes won back × £120 | ~£1,200 |
| Trade accounts (estate agents, trades, schools) | 5 accounts × £150 a month | ~£750 |
| **Total** | | **~£5,000 a month (~£60k a year)** |

Staff also save time: fewer price-check phone calls, artwork checked automatically, and proofs and collection messages sent in one tap.

## 4. Next ideas, phase 2

- **Live payments** with Stripe (Apple Pay and Google Pay) and PayPal
- **Real backend:** a database for orders and file storage for artwork (e.g. Supabase), plus automatic emails and texts at each stage
- **Online design tool:** customers type their name and details into a template and get print-ready business cards
- **Google Business Profile:** an automatic review request after each collection, plus weekly offer posts
- **New services with good margins:** van and shop-window vinyl, A-boards, printed T-shirts and workwear, wedding stationery, NHS and school contracts
- **Takeaway programme:** a reminder every 3 months to update menus, plus a delivery-app QR code and table menus
- **Direct mail:** print plus Royal Mail door-to-door leaflet drops, a high-value bundle for estate agents
- **Own domain email** (e.g. orders@kwalityprinter.co.uk) instead of Gmail

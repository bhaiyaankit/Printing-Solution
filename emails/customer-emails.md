## Sending tips

- **Consent (UK GDPR / PECR):** marketing emails (1, 6, 9 and 10) can go to existing customers about similar products, as long as every email has an **unsubscribe link** and they had a chance to opt out when they first ordered. Emails about an order (2, 3, 4 and 5) are service messages.
- **Tool:** a free plan on Mailchimp or Brevo works for the launch email. Emails 2, 3, 4 and 5 can be sent automatically by the website in phase 2.
- **Best time to send:** Tuesday to Thursday, 9–11am. For takeaways, mid-afternoon before evening service.
